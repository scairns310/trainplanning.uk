Our Projects

All of our work is displayed here. This includes our performance reports, visualisations, and future plans!

East Midlands Railway Timetable Deepdive

This part of the project focuses on East Midlands Railway timetabling information, including different service patterns, dwell times, route performance, and frequency modelling of peak and off-peak services.

CrossCountry Operational Insights

This part of the project focuses on CrossCountry Trains's Operational Insights, with a lot of work on service reliability and rolling stock utilisation. Identification of recurring patterns is key within this project.

Future Development Plans

There's a lot planned for the trainplanning project. Some things we've got lined up include dashboard previews, operator comparison tools, and real time data integration. Who knows, there may even be a possibility of a public API for real time timetabling queries...
